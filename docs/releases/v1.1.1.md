## Surfaces lock once you send

A surface the agent drew now locks after you send your answer from it, so a form can't be sent
twice by accident and it's clear your answer went through: its fields and buttons grey out and
the frame says **Sent**. To change your answer, click **Change and send again**. When the agent
updates the surface (to show a result, say), it unlocks by itself. A button that only opens a link
doesn't lock anything.
