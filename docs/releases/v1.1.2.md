## Questions from the agent

When the agent needs to know something before it can go on, it now asks with a short form instead
of in its reply. agent86 builds the form from the agent's questions, so it always works, whatever
the model. This is `ask_user`, from agent86 1.2.2.

- **Answer in place.** The questions appear right where the agent asked, headed **? Questions from
  the agent** and the model's name, in the same frame as the other surfaces the agent draws, so
  they never look like the app's own approvals. The form takes the keyboard at once. A question
  with options always offers **Other**, for your own words. **Send answers** stays greyed until
  every question not marked (optional) has an answer.
- **Or don't.** **Dismiss** (or **Esc**) closes the questions without answering; the agent carries
  on with what it knows and doesn't ask the same questions again in that turn.
- **A record of what you said.** The form stays in the conversation, locked, marked **Answered**
  or **Dismissed**, and the **? Questions** line above it opens to your answers as plain text. When
  you reopen a session, that line comes back with your answers.
- **In another tab**, a notification says the agent has questions for you.

## Also

- Any surface the agent waits on now takes the keyboard when it appears, and **Esc** dismisses it.
- agent86-core 1.2.2: forms the agent draws itself are more forgiving of a model's small mistakes,
  and broken tool calls are explained better to the model. Sessions, memory, keys, and settings are
  unchanged and still shared with the agent86 CLI.
