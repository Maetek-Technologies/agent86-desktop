The agent can now show you a form, a choice, or a confirmation instead of asking in prose, and get a
structured answer back. This is **agent86UI**, agent86's implementation of Google's
[A2UI](https://a2ui.org) protocol (agent86 is not affiliated with or endorsed by Google), from
agent86 1.2.0.

## Surfaces the agent draws

- **Forms, choices, and more.** Text fields, check boxes, options to pick from, sliders, dates,
  buttons, tabs, lists, pictures: the agent describes them and the app draws them with its own
  widgets, in the conversation where the agent asked. Nothing the model writes runs as code.
- **Always marked.** Every surface is drawn inside a dashed, blue-tinted frame headed **⧉ Drawn by
  the agent** and the model's name, so you can tell it apart from the app's own approvals, limits,
  and secret prompts, which never look like it.
- **Answer now or later.** Press a surface's button and your answer becomes your next message. Press
  it while the agent is working and it is sent when the turn ends. When the agent cannot go on
  without your answer, the frame says it is waiting; answer there, or **Dismiss** to let it carry
  on.
- **Links ask first.** A surface's link opens in your browser only after you have seen the whole
  address and agreed, and only `http` and `https` links open.
- **No tracking through media.** Pictures, video, and sound load only from files inside the
  workspace; nothing is fetched from the web.
- **Back where you left them.** Reopening a session brings its surfaces back as they were. The app
  stores nothing new: agent86 rebuilds them from the session.

See **Surfaces the agent draws** in the user guide.
